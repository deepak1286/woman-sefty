Essential Safety Tips

1. Stay Aware of Your Surroundings

Avoid using headphones or looking at your phone while walking in isolated areas.

2. Keep Your Tools Accessible

Pepper spray or alarms should be in your hand or outer pocket, not at the bottom of your bag.

3. Trust Your Instincts

If a person or place feels unsafe, leave immediately. Your intuition is your best defense.

4.Online Safety Tip for Women.

Never share personal information like your home address, live location, passwords, OTPs, or private photos with strangers online — even if they seem friendly or trustworthy.
